ABOUT

A career across public administration, water and environmental policy.

I have worked in public administration at district, state and national levels, with a particular focus on water governance, public programmes, policy implementation and institutional development.

My professional experience includes work with the National Jal Jeevan Mission, the Department of Personnel & Training, the Government of Maharashtra and district administration.

My academic journey has taken me from nautical science and maritime experience to public policy and environmental science. At Yale School of the Environment, I developed a deeper interest in the relationship between environmental systems, economics, data and public policy.

I am particularly interested in how public investments can translate into durable social and environmental outcomes.
